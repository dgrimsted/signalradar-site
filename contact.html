Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some questions we are hearing and we hope these answers help you.

Why do we describe SignalRadar as a tool that offers Business Radar?

SignalRadar has pioneered a unique approach that turns relevant news into signals used to identify disruptions that drive SignalRadar's Business Radar. By articulating the forces and trends that shape a companies plans and then finding potential disruptions of these key drivers, we provide inputs to your executive team or agents to anticipate changes while they are emerging. This equips your company with an unprecedented grasp of the near future, providing additional guidance to enable alternate scenarios and plans to be considered in a proactive manner.

What are SignalRadar's Plan Risk Signals?

Everyone who has ever created a forecast knows that even the best forecasts leave a portion of the variance observed in the category of 'unexplained'. This is part of what ends up surprising us, or better said making our forecasts 'drift'. This influence of external factors is something SignalRadar addresses by including hundreds of thousands of relevant external time series data trends that we incorporate into our forecast modeling approach. However, we also know that the world is not a static place. Forces that might bend the trajectory of any forecast often appear first in the stream of news that is any business persons constant companion. So, SignalRadar uses AI generated predictors to search global news for relevance. We then use GenAI to assess each filtered news item for degree of potential impact to your specific forecast. Plus we explain WHY each news item could matter to your metric. We then create a plan risk score that provides direction and strength of the potential for recent news to bend the trajectory of your forecast – giving you the foresight to anticipate disruptions and act on insights others may miss.

What makes SignalRadar unique?

Managing the impacts of external forces on Key Metrics is complex. SignalRadar's AI capabilities illuminate these influences, providing a clear understanding for strategic decision-making. Our forecasts are transparent and we explain factors we identified that drove them. BUT ... we go a step further and scan news events for relevant items that are presented to you as a risk scores that might 'upset the apple cart' of your plans. SignalRadar lets you transition from conventional predictive modeling to a more comprehensive understanding of your entire business environment. Our platform hosts millions of curated exogenous signals and our advanced AI algorithms produce a structural analysis of the relevant forces influencing key business metrics. This innovative approach provides Business Radar through the lens of your unique business performance metrics, enabling a more fully informed perspective and enhanced decision making.

How often is the SignalRadar database updated?

Data sources all have a time dimension, the most common frequencies of update being either daily, weekly or monthly with certain sources having other variations as well. As new data is made available our pipelines update the databases so we are refreshing continually. We routinely update the trend detection monitoring we perform on the most recent data available for each feature. Since our standard approach is to reforecast customer key performance metrics each week the structural influences we uncover reflect the most current data available.

What data sources are represented in the SignalRadar database?

Our platform hosts millions of curated data signals provided by hundreds of data providers. We are constantly increasing the sources and providers we access, a majority of the sources are 'public', created by governmental or industry organizations and other providers who offer their data signals to help people better understand their world. Our sources expand as new data signals are integrated.

How Do I Get Started With SignalRadar?

First, think about the key performance metrics you want to begin with. These should be measures you suspect are influenced by external factors. The format needs to be some sort of a time series measure, on a daily, weekly (preferred), monthly or other time scale. Our preference is for a minimum of 3 years of history (if available) to permit our engines to examine influencing factors over several seasonal cycles. A simple CSV with two columns, a date and the value for the measure is all that we need to begin. Contact us using the email form above to arrange for us to receive your data and start the process.

How can I access the plan risk scores, forecasts, news and guidance you offer?

Experience insights and guidance via Business Radar delivered by our SignalRadar app and/or by accessing our platform. Many executives begin with our iPhone app as this gives them quick and easy access to the insights they desire. The platform allows users to navigate through extensive drill-down capabilities and analytic tools. Using API's we can incorporate insights and guidance directly into your existing business intelligence platform.
