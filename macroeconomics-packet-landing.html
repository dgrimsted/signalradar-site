The Macroeconomics Packet tracks the broad financial-condition signals that sit upstream of every other report — inflation expectations, global shipping demand, dollar strength, and the labor market — providing 16-week projections that reflect advance notice of shifting planning assumptions and ever-changing news impacts.

These free reports are simply examples. To show how SignalRadar connects external signals to a real operating business, we use a fictional company, Acme Engine, as the narrative anchor throughout. Macro conditions don't show up as a single cost line for Acme the way steel or diesel do — they sit underneath the whole plan, shaping borrowing costs, contract terms, and demand across every market Acme serves. This packet shows how SignalRadar surfaces that kind of upstream signal too, not just direct commodity exposure.
