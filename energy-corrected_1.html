The Energy Packet tracks the external signals that move crude oil and refined fuel prices — Brent and WTI crude, RBOB gasoline, heating oil, natural gas, diesel, and retail gasoline — providing 16-week projections that reflect advance notice of shipping disruptions, production shocks, and ever-changing news impacts.

These free reports are simply examples. To show how SignalRadar connects external signals to a real operating business, we use a fictional company, Acme Engine, as the narrative anchor throughout. Energy touches Acme in two directions at once: as a direct cost of running its own operations, and as a demand signal for the diesel and natural gas engines it sells into freight, construction, and industrial markets. You can imagine what SignalRadar could surface using your own key metrics and company context.
